### Extra point: confirmation of restated bounds (RESULTS.md N1 & N2)

**CONFIRMED.** Both restated bounds are mathematically exact from the implementation and tightly asserted by the property and pinned tests — not merely loosened to pass:

1. **N2 — Hash cost (`clustered-hash-cost`, C31):**
   - **From the code (`internal/merkle/diff.go:149,195-199,259`):** `Session` counts `1` root hash sent at level `0`, every non-zero remote child hash received at each mismatched inner node (`0 <= level < Depth`), and every remote entry received in each mismatched leaf (`level == Depth`). Because `d` differing keys can lie on at most `d` paths at each level, the exact count is `1 + Σ(non-empty remote children of mismatched inner nodes) + Σ(remote entries of mismatched leaves) <= 1 + 16·d·Depth + d·LeafLoad(remote)`. Equality is attainable whenever all 16 siblings along each differing path are populated and mismatched leaves are at `LeafLoad`.
   - **Tightness of assertions:** `helpers_test.go:173-210,257` (`expectedShape` / `checkShape`) recomputes the exact hash count independently from the two maps alone and asserts **exact equality** (`res.Cost.Hashes == sh.hashes`) on every run of `TestPropDiffComplete`, `TestPropDiffCost`, `TestPropLongKeyDiffShape`, `FuzzMerkleDiff`, and `TestDiffBoundsPinned`. `checkCost` (`helpers_test.go:286-292`) additionally asserts `<= 1 + 16·d·Depth + d·LeafLoad(remote)`. `bounds_test.go:161-187` pins C31's exact crossing of the old `16·d·(Depth+1) = 48` bound at `n = 45` (`48` hashes) vs `n = 46` (`49` hashes).

2. **N1 — Round-trip cost (`sim-prefix-reply-tight-bound`, C21; `batched-round-trip-bound`, C12):**
   - **Honest responder (`Tree.Answer`, `diff.go:97-137`):** Equal roots take `1` round trip; unequal roots take `Depth + L` round trips (`1` per inner level `0..Depth-1`, plus `L >= 1` leaf replies). All `F` mismatched leaves fit in one reply iff `S + 2 + uvarintLen(F) <= MaxReplyBytes` (where `S` is the sum of per-leaf `uvarint(count) + entries`). When `L >= 2`, `packLeaves` stops early only when the next entry (`<= 1,058 B`, varint growth `<= 1 B`) fails to fit, so every non-final reply has encoded length `>= MaxReplyBytes - 1,059 = 1,047,517 B` (accounting for at most `1 B` difference between `uvarintLen(len(req.paths))` and `uvarintLen(len(resp.leaves))`), carrying at least `1,047,517 - 4 = 1,047,513 B` of leaf body with at most `2 B` of extra count-varint overhead per cut (`>= 1,047,511 B` of `S` per non-final reply). Hence `L <= ceil(S / 1,047,511) <= ceil(S / 1,047,510)`. The divisor `1,047,510` in `helpers_test.go:139,248-250` is tight to within `1` byte out of `1 MiB` (for a full 4,096 × 1,024-byte leaf, `ceil(4,333,570 / 1,047,510) = 5` and actual `L = 5`).
