## New Findings

1. **NIT — `scrubShard` deleting a shard from `inv.actual` on `env.ErrIO` triggers rebuild loops and cannot rewrite an unreadable shard (contradicting `build-notes.md` section 15).**
   - **Location:** `internal/node/inventory.go:166-172` (`scrubShard`), interacting with `internal/storage/chunkstore.go:92-108` (`ChunkStore.Put`) and `internal/node/inventory.go:357-360` (`stateAbsent`); documented inaccurately in `agent_work/06-integration/build-notes.md:1247, 1268-1269`.
   - **What happens:**
     1. When a shard suffers persistent `env.ErrIO` on reads — even when `k` other holders are healthy — `scrubShard` deletes `name` from `inv.actual`, so `checkInventory` launches a full network `rebuild` on every maintenance tick. `rebuild` fetches all `N-1` peers, decodes and re-encodes the stripe, and calls `n.store.Store().Put(id, enc[es.idx])` (`inventory.go:513`). `ChunkStore.Put` calls `old, err := s.read(id)` (`chunkstore.go:92`) first and returns `err` on any error other than `ErrNotFound` (`chunkstore.go:106-108`), never reaching `s.disk.Write`. Thus a rebuild **never** rewrites an `ErrIO` shard and instead loops `RebuildFailures++` on every `MaintainPeriod` tick indefinitely while `ErrIO` persists.
     2. When a shard on a stripe with `< k` available peers (e.g. `k=2, m=0`) suffers a **single** transient `env.ErrIO`, `scrubShard` deletes `name` from `inv.actual` and advances `inv.cursor`. `rebuild` fails (`< k` peers) and calls `stateAbsent`, which reads `name` cleanly from disk, verifies its checksum matches (`inventory.go:358`), and returns without restoring `inv.actual`. `checkInventory` then launches a doomed network `rebuild` (`RebuildFailures++`) on every maintenance tick until the scrub cursor wraps around every other shard in the bucket.
     Safety is unaffected (zero false absence records, zero false declarations, intact bytes untouched, GETs succeed whenever `k` peers answer).
   - **Reproduction:** Observed in `/tmp/neon2c-src` via `TestNeon2cQ1_ObserveKeptScrubRebuildBehaviorUnderReadError`:
     - `persistent_ErrIO_with_k_healthy_peers` (`k=2, m=1`, 3 healthy holders, `FailReads(h0, n0, 100)`, 10 s at `MaintainPeriod=1s`): `Rebuilds=0 RebuildFailures=10 AbsentRecords=0`.
     - `single_transient_ErrIO_with_many_objects_m0` (`k=2, m=0`, 20 objects, `InventoryScrub=1`, `FailReads(holder, targetShard, 1)`, 25 s): `Rebuilds=0 RebuildFailures=10 AbsentRecords=0`.
   - **Smallest fix:** In `scrubShard` (`internal/node/inventory.go:167-172`), delete `name` from `inv.actual` only when `isNotFound(err) || isCorrupt(err)` (returning without modifying `inv.actual` on transient/persistent `env.ErrIO`, so already-indexed intact shards do not trigger rebuild loops). Update `agent_work/06-integration/build-notes.md:1247` and `:1268-1269` to remove the claim that a rebuild rewrites a shard while disk reads keep returning `env.ErrIO`.
