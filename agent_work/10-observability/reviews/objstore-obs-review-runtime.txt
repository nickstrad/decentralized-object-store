Findings
1. NIT cmd/objnode/main.go:~220 (`<-runnerDone`): the runner join is unbounded by sctx. It relies on every adapter honouring the
   Publish context (built-in ones do; the runner's own per-cycle timeout also bounds a cooperative adapter). A future non-cooperative
   adapter could hold shutdown past ShutdownTimeout. Suggest `select { case <-runnerDone: case <-sctx.Done(): }` and report ctx error.
   Reproducer: register an adapter whose Publish ignores ctx, enable it, send SIGTERM mid-publish.
